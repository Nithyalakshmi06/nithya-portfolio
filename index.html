Project 02

Satellite Telemetry Anomaly Detection

Problem
Multivariate satellite telemetry drifts in ways a threshold rule misses, and abnormal behaviour can signal a developing fault.
Solution
An LSTM Autoencoder trained on NASA's SMAP telemetry dataset that learns normal behaviour and flags sequences by reconstruction error, wrapped in a Streamlit dashboard.
PythonTensorFlowLSTM AutoencoderStreamlit

Key outcome —full pipeline engineered end to end: loading, preprocessing, training, anomaly scoring and visualisation.

From problem to solution

The sequence I work through on every project, whether it's a pipeline or a model.

01

Understand the problem

What is actually being asked, and what would a good answer look like.

02

Collect & prepare data

Source it, clean it, validate it, and make the shape explicit.

03

Build the solution

Pipeline, model, or application — whichever the problem calls for.

04

Evaluate & improve

Measure it honestly, then tune features, parameters and structure.

05

Deploy & visualise

Put it somewhere it can be used, and make the result readable.

06

Learn & iterate

Take what the build taught me into the next one.
